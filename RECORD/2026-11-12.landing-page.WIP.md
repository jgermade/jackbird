# Landing page on GitHub Pages

## 2026-11-12 20:40 — The landing at the root, the demo in /demo/, the design system in /design/

**Asked by the owner.** GitHub Pages served the app itself at <https://jgermade.github.io/jackbird/>.
Instead: an eye-catching landing page there, and the demo at
<https://jgermade.github.io/jackbird/demo/>. Then: a route to see the design system.

**What there is now.**

- `landing/`: a static page (HTML, CSS, a small script; no build step, no framework), drawn with
  the Deck theme's own tokens so it is the same front panel as the app:
  - a sticky faceplate with the wordmark in raised metal (the same technique as the app's), the
    sections, and a key that switches Silver / Graphite (remembered; the system's tone until then);
  - the hero: the pitch, "Try the demo" (the orange key) and "Download" (the latest release), and
    a slab of the deck tilted towards the reader, its display lit, a marquee running and a
    spectrum dancing (still under `prefers-reduced-motion`);
  - the app's screenshot in a window, Silver or Graphite with the page's tone;
  - six feature modules bolted to the panel (LED, printed label, heading);
  - the phone, the design system (a palette card drawn from the live tokens) and the four ways to
    get it (browser, desktop, service, Android);
  - the tab's icon lit like the display, as in the app (`src/lib/favicon.ts`).
- `landing/design/`: a redirect to `../demo/theme/default/`, the Deck theme's specimen sheet,
  which every build already carries (`vite.config.ts`, the `theme` entry). So the design system on
  the site is always the released one.
- `scripts/build-landing.mjs [out]`: puts the page together in a folder (default `.tmp/landing`),
  copying in what it borrows instead of duplicating it in the repository: `theme/default/tokens.css`,
  `fonts.css` and `fonts/`, the logo, `docs/screenshots/`.
- `deploy.yml`: checks out the repository, builds the landing into `site/`, and unzips the release
  into `site/demo/`. The app's relative base (`./`) makes it work there unchanged.
- `docs/releases.md` describes the three paths.

**Checked.** Served locally (`.tmp/landing/`), screenshots at 1366px and 390px in both tones: no
horizontal scroll (the display's marquee needed its grid track at `minmax(0, 1fr)`), the tone key
swaps the page, the screenshot and the favicon.

**Not checked here.** The deploy itself: it runs on GitHub, on the next deploy of a release.

## 2026-11-12 22:15 — The images are the real interface

Asked by the owner: the landing's images must be the real UI. The hero's deck was a mock-up (a
display and keys drawn in the page's own CSS, with a fake marquee and spectrum).

- **`scripts/screenshots.cjs`** (committed, unlike the two generators before it, lost with their
  sessions' `.tmp/`): builds the made-up library of the earlier screenshots (Ines Halloway, Kestrel
  Lane, Mono Harbour, Orla Venn, The Quiet Parade; covers drawn on a canvas; tones, overtones and
  pink noise from ffmpeg, so the spectrum moves; tagged by ffmpeg), copies it into the page's OPFS
  behind `showDirectoryPicker`, plays it in the real app and captures `docs/screenshots/`:
  `library-silver.png`, `library-graphite.png`, `edit-dialog.png` (three tracks), `phone.png`, and
  for the landing `faceplate-silver.png` / `faceplate-graphite.png` (the faceplate's deck: wordmark,
  display, keys). `node scripts/screenshots.cjs [app URL]`, with the dev server running.
- **The hero** now shows that faceplate, in the page's tone, leaning back like a unit's front over
  the J-card stripes; the pitch sits above it in two columns. The mock's CSS and the script's
  spectrum are gone.
- The README's screenshots, made by the same run, show today's interface (the wordmark, the lit
  play key, the taller spectrum).
